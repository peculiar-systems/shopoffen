(Directive (EU) 2019/882) as implemented in {COUNTRY}'s national law ({NATIONAL LAW}).

- all functions of the shop can be used with a keyboard alone;
- images that carry information have text alternatives;
- text and important controls have sufficient colour contrast;
- form fields are labelled, and errors are explained in words;
- the pages can be zoomed to 200 % and read on narrow screens without loss of content;
